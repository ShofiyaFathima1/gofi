# Audio Instructions

To play "Poraney Poraney" on your scrapbook timeline website:
1. Place your legally obtained audio file named `poraney-poraney.mp3` directly in this `assets/audio/` folder.
   (The website looks for `assets/audio/poraney-poraney.mp3` by default).
2. Alternatively, you can click "Choose Audio File" directly on the website's music player card to load your audio file instantly from your computer!
